### 2. The 4D Mental Health Model (Core Framework)

The website will be structured around four interconnected dimensions:

1. **Mind (Psychology & Therapy)**
   - **Highlights**: Professional Hypnotherapy (primary focus), Psychotherapy, and Psychological assessments.
   - **Goal**: Reversing anxiety and depression through deep-rooted behavioral and subconscious interventions.

2. **Neuro (Brain Science)**
   - **Highlights**: QEEG Brain Mapping and Neurofeedback Therapy.
   - **Technology**: Utilizing industry-standard BrainMaster equipment for objective brain data.
   - **Goal**: Monitoring and training brainwave patterns for peak performance and mental stability.

3. **Body (Functional Physiology)**
   - **Highlights**: Functional Medicine and Nutrition.
   - **Focus**: Identifying physical imbalances (e.g., Cortisol/Hormone issues, Thyroid, Post-partum) that trigger mental symptoms.
   - **Goal**: Addressing the biological foundations of mental health.

4. **Energy (Bioenergetics)**
   - **Highlights**: Bio-Well/Cell Wellbeing analysis.
   - **Focus**: Subtle core energy, meridian systems, and chakra balancing.
   - **Goal**: Correcting energetic imbalances before they manifest as physical or mental distress.

### 3. Proposed Website Strategy

*   **Design Aesthetic**: "Premium Clinical Meets Holistic Wellness." High-quality imagery, clean typography, and a calming color palette (Blues, Teals, and Gold accents).
